Allmyles proudly presents its proprietary merchant payment mechanism in cooperation with Barion and Ixaris. Allmyles just launched Allmyles Double Clutch Payment System that enables global reconciliation of payments between merchants and travel service providers seamlessly.

Start Allmyles API 30-day-free trial with registering your card at the Barion Smart Gateway. You can easly use your our staging system to assess your technical and human resources. We only charge you 30 days after the registration and the support fee while development is no more than 20 EUR per month. After you are ready with the integration of Allmyles Travel API, you can go for a certification of your app and we switch you into production. To really start using live environment a commercial deposit is required that is charged against your card registered in our dashboard using Barion Smart Gateway.

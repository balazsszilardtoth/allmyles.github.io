Allmyles Travel APIs are being enriched continuously but Airline Direct Connect integration is a milestone for us. This new connection does however take baby steps, it will be an asset for major airlines, no matter if they are Full Service Carriers (FSC) or Low Cost Carriers (LCC) to let developers and aggregators reach their content directly without pre-internet middlemen, like GDSs and Airline Inventory providers. Whit Allmyles Airline Direct Connect you can book tha same content, plus web-only fares and merchandising like onboard services or ancillaries. It is promised to have all deal breaker parameters in search results thus avoiding unexpected price changes over user journey.

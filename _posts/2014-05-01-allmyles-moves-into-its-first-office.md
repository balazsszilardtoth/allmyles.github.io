Allmyles Ltd moves in its first office in the city centre of Budapest - near Gozsdu Udvar that is situated in a lively and vibrant quarter of the city. Allmyles first office is surrounded by great restaurants, coffe shops and is becoming popular among party people from all over the world. The office is only a stone's throw from local startups like Prezi.com, Tresorit and LogMeIn.
